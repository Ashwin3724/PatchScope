HOW IT WORKS

One patch at a time

Each 16 × 16 patch becomes four simple texture features. Its distance to the nearest normal reference patch becomes an anomaly score. The largest patch score becomes the image score.

This is a classical feature baseline, not PatchCore, not a pretrained neural network, and not a trained defect classifier. See the interview guide and audit in the ZIP.

READING THE MAP

Signal, not proof

Lighting, resizing, unusual textures and color differences can change a score. Inspect the original image and validate with representative samples before drawing conclusions.
